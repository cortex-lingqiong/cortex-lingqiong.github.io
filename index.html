我们守的原则What we hold to

做带记忆的 AI，信任比功能重要。For an AI that remembers, trust matters more than features.

记忆归你Your memories are yours

你能看到它记住了什么、从哪次对话得来；你要删，就是真的删掉。You can see what it remembers and which conversation it came from. When you delete something, it is gone.

不编造No made-up memories

没发生过的事不说成发生过。被问到不知道的，直接说不知道。我们每周测一次这一条。It never claims something happened when it didn't. Asked about something it doesn't know, it says it doesn't know. We test this every week.

要紧的事你拍板You decide what matters

发消息、删除、花钱这类事，AI 只能提出来，要你书面确认才会做。Sending, deleting, spending: the AI can propose these, and acts only after you confirm in writing.
