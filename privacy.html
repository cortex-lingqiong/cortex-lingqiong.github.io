Privacy Policy

Effective date: 2026-11-16 · Operator: Cortex 灵穹创新 (incorporation in progress; legal name and address will be published here once registered)

中文摘要：我们只收集提供服务所需的信息；你的记忆属于你，可随时查看、导出和删除；我们不出售个人信息，也不用你的记忆训练给别人用的模型。

1. What we collect

  • Account details: the name and email you give us.
  • Conversations and memories: what you say to Ling (text or voice) and the memories Ling keeps so it can remember you.
  • Technical data: basic logs (time, IP address, errors) needed to keep the service running and secure.
  • Payment data: handled by our payment processor. We do not see or store full card numbers.

2. How we use it

To provide the service, remember what you asked it to remember, keep it secure, answer support requests and bill for paid plans. We do not sell personal information, and we do not use your memories to train models offered to other customers.

3. Your control

You can see what Ling remembers and where each memory came from, export it, and delete any memory or your whole account. Deletion removes the data from our active systems; backups roll over within 30 days. Team-edition data stays on the customer's own hardware and is not sent to us.

4. Who else processes data

Hosting and payment providers that process data for us under contract. We disclose data to authorities only when the law requires it.

5. Retention and security

We keep data while your account is active and delete it after you close it, except where the law requires us to keep records. Data is encrypted in transit; access is limited to people who need it to run the service.

6. Children

The service is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their data.

7. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example California or the EU), you may have rights to access, correct, delete or port your data and to object to certain processing. Contact us to use them; we reply within 30 days.

8. Changes and contact

We will post changes here and update the effective date. Questions: contact us through the contact channels on our homepage (X @innovation641 or LinkedIn).
